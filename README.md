A dedicated study guide and professional reference for candidates preparing for the **APMG International Better Business Cases Practitioner Certification**, focused on applying the Five Case Model to real-world projects and investment proposals.

---

## Exam Overview

The Better Business Cases Practitioner qualification validates the ability to apply the Five Case Model and develop a structured business case for a project or investment proposal.

- **Exam Format**: Open-book objective testing based on a case-study scenario.
- **Target Audience**: Project and programme managers, business case practitioners, investment appraisal professionals, specialist advisers, and executives involved in reviewing or approving proposals.
- **Prerequisites**: A pass in the Better Business Cases Foundation examination is required.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Overview of the Five Case Model

- Purpose, structure, and underlying principles of the Five Case Model.
- Relationships between the five cases throughout business case development.
- Business case lifecycle and its connection with project and programme management.

### 2. Developing the Strategic Case

- Establishing the case for change and strategic fit.
- Defining SMART objectives, expected benefits, and outcomes.
- Identifying business needs, risks, constraints, and dependencies.

### 3. Developing the Economic Case

- Applying options framework analysis to develop longlists and shortlists.
- Assessing costs, benefits, risks, and alternative solutions.
- Identifying the preferred option and demonstrating value for money.

### 4. Developing the Commercial Case

- Determining an appropriate procurement and commercial strategy.
- Considering commercial arrangements and allocation of risks.
- Establishing suitable payment mechanisms and supplier arrangements.

### 5. Developing the Financial Case

- Assessing affordability and financial viability.
- Identifying funding requirements and securing relevant funds.
- Evaluating capital requirements and the financial impact of proposals.

### 6. Developing the Management Case

- Defining project management and change control arrangements.
- Establishing benefits measurement and risk management arrangements.
- Planning implementation, monitoring, and post-project evaluation.
